Every buyer and seller knows where their sale stands, every week.

People are far more at ease in a long process when they can see where they are in it. The weekly roadmap is built around that: each step of the sale, what happens next and who to call, sent on the same day every week in your branding.

What happens next?

With enquiries satisfied, both solicitors can now prepare for exchange. The next step is to propose a completion date that suits everyone in the chain. Once a date is agreed, contracts can be exchanged, which is the point at which the sale becomes legally binding. We are chasing at least once a week and will let you know as soon as a date is proposed.
